Management Guide

This page lets you manage your products, categories, and deals.
Everything saves automatically when you edit fields or click buttons.
SECTIONS
Sections are a broad grouping above categories — e.g. "Pizzas" groups "Traditional Pizzas" and "Loaded Pizzas".
Create sections first, then assign categories to them.
Sections appear as "Any <Section>" options in the deal bundle builder.
CATEGORIES
Add a category and optionally assign it to a section.
Edit a category by clicking its name and typing.
Change its section using the dropdown in the same row.
Delete a category using the × button.
PRODUCTS
Fill in the form to create a new product.
Title, price, description, and category are required.
Upload an image if needed.
Click Add Product to save.
VARIANTS (RANKING)
Enable Ranking to create multiple price options.
Example: Small, Medium, Large.
Each rank has a name and price.
When ranking is enabled, the base price is ignored.
Rank 1 becomes the fallback price.
EDITING PRODUCTS
All product fields are editable directly in the cards.
Click into any field and change the value.
Click outside the field to save automatically.
Category changes save instantly.
Use the × button to delete a product.
DEALS
Deals are bundles of existing products.
They do not change the original products.
CREATING A DEAL
Enter title, price, and optional description.
Choose a deal type (Deal or Hot Deal).
Upload an image if needed.
ADDING PRODUCTS TO A DEAL
Click Add Product Form.
Select a product and quantity.
If the product has ranking, select a variant.
Repeat to add multiple items.
BUNDLE PREVIEW
The preview shows exactly what is included.
Example: 2 × Burger (Large), 1 × Fries.
This updates automatically.
EDITING DEALS
All deal fields can be edited directly.
Changes auto-save after typing or selection.
You can also click Save Deal.
REMOVING ITEMS FROM DEALS
Click × on a product row to remove it.
At least one row will always remain.
DELETING DEALS
Click the × button on a deal card.
Confirm to delete permanently.
NOTES
All changes are saved to the server.
If something fails, you will see an alert.
Refreshing will reload all data.
