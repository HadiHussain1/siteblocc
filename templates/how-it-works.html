How it works

From restaurant idea to working website in one guided flow.

Dinebloc asks the right questions, builds the site structure, generates AI visuals and copy, and gives the owner a dashboard for the daily work.

01

Describe the restaurant

Add the business name, slogan, location, phone, colours, story, and a short description. This becomes the source material for the site and AI generation.

02

Choose the modules

Pick what the business actually needs: online ordering, catering, reservations, staff tools, delivery support, admin controls, and customer management.

03

Let AI prepare the visual layer

Dinebloc generates a relevant hero image, a polished featured section, and restaurant-specific copy designed to feel custom rather than pasted on.

04

Import or build the menu

Owners can add products manually or upload a menu file. The AI importer extracts categories, products, prices, details, and ranking variants.

05

Run the site from the dashboard

After launch, the restaurant can manage orders, products, deals, customers, workers, bookings, analytics, and site settings from one place.

What makes it different

Restaurant features are part of the core build.

Less manual setup: AI helps prepare visuals, featured copy, descriptions, and menu products.

Less maintenance pain: owners can change products, deals, and categories without calling a developer.

Less wasted money: independent restaurants get practical tools without paying for a slow custom agency build.
